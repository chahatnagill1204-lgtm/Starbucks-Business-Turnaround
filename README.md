# ☕ Starbucks Business Turnaround & Performance Diagnostic

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes Starbucks' business performance from FY2021–FY2025 to identify the key drivers of profitability deterioration and develop a data-backed turnaround strategy.

The analysis combines SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI to move from financial diagnosis to actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Question

> Why did Starbucks' profitability deteriorate despite continued revenue and store growth, and what actions could management take to restore performance?

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Revenue increased from approximately **$29.1B to $37.2B** between FY2021 and FY2025.
- Operating income declined from approximately **$4.87B to $2.94B**.
- Operating margin compressed from approximately **16.8% to 7.9%**.
- Starbucks expanded its store network from approximately **33.8K to 41.0K stores**.
- Customer transaction weakness became an important driver of comparable-store performance deterioration.
- Store productivity weakened after peaking around FY2023.
- FY2025 included significant restructuring and impairment charges.

## 🧠 Analytical Approach

### 2. Demand Diagnostics

- North America performance

### 3. Store Productivity

Analyzed:

- Store count
- Store growth
- Revenue per store

### 4. Turnaround Strategy

Developed recommendations around:

- Customer traffic recovery
- Store productivity
- Operational simplification
- Market-specific strategies

## 💡 Turnaround Recommendations

### 1. Restore Customer Traffic

Improve customer experience, peak-hour throughput and visit frequency.

### 2. Improve Store Productivity

Optimize labor deployment, identify underperforming locations and improve revenue per store.

### 3. Simplify Operations

Reduce operational complexity and improve order-flow efficiency.

### 4. Use Market-Specific Strategies

Prioritize traffic recovery in North America while addressing ticket and competitive dynamics in China.
